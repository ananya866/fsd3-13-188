BrightPath Tuition Website
===========================

Files:
- index.html (home page)
- registration.html
- login.html
- courses.html
- feedback.html
- price-details.html
- style.css

Open index.html in a browser. All pages are connected through the navbar and footer.
Font Awesome icons are loaded from a CDN, so an internet connection is needed for the icons.

Note: Forms are front-end demos only. They do not save submissions or authenticate users without a backend.
Update the sample tuition fees and branding as needed.
